Save Every Drop, Secure Every Future.

Hassan district depends on monsoon-fed reservoirs, lakes and household tanks. Uneven rainfall, leaking pipelines and unmanaged storage can leave neighbourhoods short in summer. AquaRakshak helps communities track tank levels, cut wastage, harvest rainwater and report problems early.

Conservation Tips

🔧 Fix dripping taps and leaking pipes quickly.
🪣 Reuse washing water for plants.
🚿 Choose short showers and close taps while brushing.
🌧️ Install rooftop harvesting before the monsoon.
🚰 Cover tanks and check float valves to stop overflow.
👥 Share what you learn with neighbours and schools.
